Community Guide

Create an Account

Register using your official CUET student email. Verify your account using the OTP sent to your email. Complete your profile.

List a Tool

List your tool with the details students need. Only the owner can edit their own tools. Edits affect only future rentals.

ImagesConditionRental PricePickup LocationDescription

Request a Rental

Browse available tools. You cannot rent your own tool. Your name and student ID are filled automatically. Send your rental request.

Chat Before Renting

Use the built-in messaging system to discuss availability, pickup and questions before accepting the rental.

Owner Approval

The owner reviews the request. If accepted, they schedule the pickup date, pickup time and pickup location. You'll receive a notification to review these details.

Pickup Day

At the scheduled pickup time, both users confirm the tool was handed over, the tool was received and the 40% advance payment was completed. Only after BOTH users confirm does the rental officially begin.

During the Rental

Track everything from Rental History. Automatic reminders help ensure on-time returns.

ProgressTime RemainingRental StatusPayment Status

Returning the Tool

When the rental period ends, both users confirm the return. If any issue occurs, either user may report the other. Reports do NOT cancel rentals automatically.

Leave a Review

After a successful rental, both participants may leave one review. Your reputation is built through ratings, reviews and successful rentals.

Shared Values

Respect Others

Treat borrowed tools as if they were your own.

Be Honest

Pay fairly. Return on time. Describe tools accurately.

Build Your Reputation

Helpful reviews and successful rentals make you a trusted community member.

Let's build a trusted sharing community together!
