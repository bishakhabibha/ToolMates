Why ToolMates?

Save Money

Borrow tools only when needed instead of purchasing expensive equipment.

Share Resources

Many tools sit unused most of the year. ToolMates helps them reach students who actually need them.

Sustainable Campus

Sharing reduces unnecessary purchases and promotes responsible resource use.

Earn Extra Income

Turn your idle tools into useful campus resources while earning rental income.

Trusted Community

Ratings, reviews, rental history and reports help everyone make informed decisions.

Convenient & Simple

Everything happens in one place. Browse, chat, rent, track, review.
